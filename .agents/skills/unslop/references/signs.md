# Signs of slop & Craft Fixes

## Surfaces and color
- Purple to blue or violet to pink gradients on backgrounds, cards, buttons → one neutral surface, accent only where it means something
- Gradient text in headings, numbers or logo → solid primary text color
- Colored glows, drop-shadow on emoji or glowing pills → remove, depth from surface steps and hairlines
- Radial color washes or blurred blobs behind content → remove
- Neon or high contrast colored borders → hairline at 6 to 10% opacity
- Many near identical tints of the same hue for text → three text roles from one neutral ramp
- Glass cards with white inner highlight and colored shadow → plain raised surface with a hairline

## Type
- Headings at weight 800 or 900 → 400 to 600 and size for hierarchy
- Uppercase labels with wide tracking → sentence case at normal tracking
- Numbers in mixed formats → one format, tabular figures
- Negative letter spacing on small text → zero or slightly positive

## Icons
- Emoji as nav icons, label decoration or status → one line icon set or clean text
- Emoji inside pills and counts → plain number
- Icons in many sizes and colors → one size, one stroke, text role color

## Copy
- Exclamation marks and hype words (supercharge, seamless, unlock, revolutionize) → what the thing actually does
- Trailing periods on labels and buttons → none
- Unverified claims → quiet and specific
