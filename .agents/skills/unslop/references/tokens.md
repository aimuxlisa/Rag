# Tokens

Starting values for each pass. When the project already has a good system, keep its values and only fix how they are used.

## Spacing scale
4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64, 80, 96, 128
- Inside a control: 4 to 12
- Between related items: 2 to 8
- Between groups: 12 to 24
- Between sections: 32 to 64, landing sections 80 to 128

## Neutral ramps (Dark / Ink)
- Deepest surface: #07080d
- Page background: #0b0d14
- Card: #11141c
- Raised: #1a1e28
- Hairline: primary text at 8%
- Primary text: #eef0f5
- Secondary text: #a3a9b8
- Muted text and icons: #7a8296

## Type scale
- Small labels, meta, counts: 12 (400, mono welcome)
- Secondary text, captions: 13 to 14 (400)
- Body, nav items: 14 to 15 (400 to 500)
- Logo wordmark: 16 to 18 (500 to 600)
- Hero subhead: 18 to 20 (400, secondary color)
- Card figures: 28 to 32 (500, tabular)
- Page title: 28 to 36 (400 to 500)
- Hero headline: 56 to 72 desktop, 36 to 44 mobile (400 to 500)

## Radius
- Keycap, tag, small button: 4 to 6
- Row, input, button: 8 to 10
- Card: 12 to 16
- Panel or window: 14 to 20
Nested radius: inner = outer - padding.

## Motion
- One easing curve: cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)
- Hover, press: 120 to 180ms
- Menus, popovers: 160 to 220ms
- Entrances: 300 to 500ms
